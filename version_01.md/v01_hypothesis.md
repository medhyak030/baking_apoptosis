## Layer 1

### Question 1: TRAIL
How does TRAIL determine whether a cell undergoes apoptosis or survival?

### Working Hypothesis
The signalling outcome is not determined by TRAIL alone but by the receptor landscape and regulatory state of the target cell.


## Layer 1 

### Question 2: Receptor Architecture and Cellular Gatekeeping

### Working Hypothesis
The outcome of TRAIL signalling is determined by the regulatory architecture established by the target cell rather than by TRAIL itself. Cellular state, receptor composition, receptor expression, membrane organisation, and downstream signalling competence collectively shape the effective receptor landscape encountered by TRAIL. Consequently, the same ligand can produce either apoptotic or survival signalling depending on the molecular environment of the target cell.

## Layer 1 

### Question 3: Cellular Decision Making

### Working Hypothesis
TRAIL-mediated cellular fate is regulated through a threshold-dependent decision-making architecture rather than by ligand binding alone. Productive receptor clustering, efficient DISC assembly, and downstream signalling competence together determine whether apoptotic commitment is achieved. Alterations in receptor organisation, structural integrity, or regulatory balance are hypothesised to shift this threshold toward either apoptosis or cellular survival.
